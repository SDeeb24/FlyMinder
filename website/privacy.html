Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

FlyMinder ("the app") is a macOS menu bar utility published by FlyMinder. We respect your privacy.

Data we collect

None. FlyMinder does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app works entirely on your Mac.

Data stored on your device

FlyMinder saves your preferences locally using macOS UserDefaults, including:

  • Your custom banner message
  • Reminder interval and on/off state
  • Banner animation speed
  • Whether you have dismissed the welcome message

This data never leaves your computer.

Network access

FlyMinder does not require an internet connection to function. If you tap a link in the About window (website, privacy policy, or support email), your browser or mail app handles that separately.

Third parties

We do not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or third-party SDKs.

Children

FlyMinder is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@flyminder.app.
