✈️ FlyMinder

A friendly Mac menu bar app that flies your message across the screen so you actually get up and move.

Download for Mac

Requires macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later. Replace this link with your hosted .dmg URL.

Features

  • Custom banner message (up to 45 characters)
  • Reminders every 20, 30, 45, or 60 minutes
  • Live countdown to your next reminder
  • Floats above fullscreen apps
  • Launch at login
  • No account, no tracking, no calendar access

How it works

  1. Download and open FlyMinder.
  2. Click the airplane icon in your menu bar.
  3. Type your message, pick an interval, and hit Test reminder.
